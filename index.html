Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

Pipling is built to run entirely on your device. You do not need to create an account, sign in, or give us an email address to use the app, and we do not run servers that collect or store your personal information.

Information We Collect

Everything you enter in Pipling is saved locally on your device using the operating system's standard local storage (SharedPreferences on Android and its equivalent on iOS). This includes:

  • Child's name — optional. You can skip this entirely or keep the placeholder name; a real name is never required.
  • Age band — one of three ranges: 1–3, 4–8, or 9–13 years.
  • Pickiness level — how selective your child currently is about food.
  • Allergies and excluded foods — optional.
  • Health goals — optional.
  • Kitchen tools — which equipment you have available, such as a blender.
  • Smoothie logs — for each smoothie you record: the date and time, your child's reaction, and an optional free-text note.

None of this information is transmitted to us or to anyone else. It stays on your device.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only on your device, to:

  • Show recipe recommendations matched to your child's age band, pickiness level, allergies and excluded foods, and available kitchen tools.
  • Track and display your progress — including the Journey and Pickiness levels — based on the smoothies you log.

All of this processing happens locally on your device. Nothing is sent to an external server, and we have no way to access it.

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat. Pipling uses RevenueCat solely to manage subscriptions. When you start, restore, or manage a subscription, the information needed to complete that purchase is exchanged between the app, RevenueCat, and Apple's App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf for subscription management only. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not use any analytics tools, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or other third-party tracking or profiling technology.

Data Retention

Because your data lives only on your device, there is nothing for us to retain — we hold no copy and keep no server-side backup. If you delete Pipling from your device, all of the information listed above is permanently removed with it. Reinstalling the app starts from an empty state.
